AI Email Guardian

A personal AI-powered email monitoring and notification assistant.

About the Project

AI Email Guardian is a personal software project designed to monitor authorized Gmail accounts and identify potentially important emails.

The application uses the Gmail API, Python, Google Gemini, and Telegram to support email classification, summarization, and notifications.

Gmail access is read-only. The application does not send, modify, or delete emails.

Email information may be processed by external services when required for AI analysis and notifications. Local privacy filters are used to reduce unnecessary data sharing.

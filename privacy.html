AI Email Guardian - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Overview

AI Email Guardian is a personal email monitoring and notification application. It identifies potentially important emails and provides AI-generated summaries and Telegram notifications.

The application is intended for personal use with explicitly authorized Gmail accounts.

2. Google Account Access

AI Email Guardian uses Google's OAuth 2.0 authentication system and the Gmail API.

The application requests read-only Gmail access to retrieve email metadata and message content for classification.

It does not request permission to send, modify, or delete Gmail messages.

3. Information Processed

Depending on the message, the application may process sender information, email subjects, message content, message identifiers, and classification results.

Local filtering attempts to identify sensitive messages and reduce the information shared with external services. Such filtering cannot guarantee detection of every sensitive detail.

4. AI Processing

AI Email Guardian may transmit limited email information to the Google Gemini API to classify importance, generate summaries, and identify actions requiring attention.

The application uses local filtering and data minimization before AI processing. Data sent to Gemini is subject to Google's applicable API privacy and data-processing terms.

AI analysis is intended solely to provide the email monitoring functionality requested by the user.

5. Telegram Notifications

Important email summaries, priority information, and recommended actions may be sent to the user's authorized Telegram chat through the Telegram Bot API.

Certain sensitive messages may generate a generic notification without transferring the original email content.

6. Local Storage

The application stores OAuth credentials, authorization tokens, mailbox checkpoints, and processed message records locally in its configured environment.

The current SQLite message database stores message identifiers and processing status rather than complete email bodies.

7. Data Sharing and Usage

Google user data is accessed only to provide the authorized email monitoring, classification, and notification features.

The developer does not sell Gmail user data or use it for advertising or ad targeting.

Limited information may be transmitted to Google Gemini and Telegram as necessary to provide the requested functionality.

The application's use of Google user data is intended to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

8. Access Revocation and Deletion

Users can revoke application access through their Google Account's third-party connections settings.

Locally stored tokens and processing records can be removed from the application's configured storage environment.

Revoking access prevents future authorized Gmail API requests after the revocation takes effect.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if application functionality, data handling, or third-party integrations change.
